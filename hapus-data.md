# Penghapusan Data — UMBJM Socials

_[English version below](#data-deletion--umbjm-socials) · [Kebijakan Privasi](./)_

Aplikasi UMBJM Socials **tidak menyimpan isi komentar atau isi DM Anda**. Yang mungkin tersimpan hanyalah catatan audit balasan UMBJM kepada Anda: ID percakapan/komentar, ID pengguna Instagram Anda (khusus untuk mengirim balasan), teks balasan UMBJM, dan waktunya.

## Cara meminta penghapusan
1. Kirim email ke **humas@umbjm.ac.id** atau **contact@faridmaruf.com** dengan subjek **"Penghapusan Data UMBJM Socials"**.
2. Sebutkan nama pengguna Instagram atau nama akun Facebook Anda, dan (bila ada) tautan komentar atau perkiraan tanggal DM.
3. Kami akan menghapus catatan terkait dari aplikasi paling lambat **3 × 24 jam** sejak email diterima dan mengonfirmasi lewat email.

Komentar dan pesan yang Anda kirim tetap berada di Instagram/Facebook dan diatur oleh kebijakan Meta. Anda dapat menghapus komentar Anda sendiri di Instagram/Facebook, atau menghapus percakapan DM dari aplikasi Instagram.

# Data Deletion — UMBJM Socials

The UMBJM Socials app **does not store the content of your comments or direct messages**. The only data that may be stored is the audit record of UMBJM's replies to you: the conversation/comment ID, your Instagram-scoped user ID (used only to deliver the reply), the reply text sent by UMBJM, and its timestamp.

**How to request deletion**
1. Email **humas@umbjm.ac.id** or **contact@faridmaruf.com** with the subject **"UMBJM Socials Data Deletion"**.
2. Include your Instagram username or Facebook account name and, if available, a link to the comment or the approximate date of the message.
3. We will delete the related records from the app within **3 × 24 hours** of receiving your email and confirm by email.

Comments and messages you sent remain on Instagram/Facebook and are governed by Meta's policies. You can delete your own comments on Instagram/Facebook, or delete a DM conversation from the Instagram app.
